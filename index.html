How to play

Concept

Four words are hidden in the grid, one for each color. Read a color's tiles left to right and top to bottom, like a page, to see what it spells. The words under the grid show what each color spells right now.

Tiles are linked in groups of four called pinwheels. Each pinwheel holds one tile of each color. Turning it moves its four colors one tile clockwise. The letters never move, so one turn changes one letter in every word.

Rules

  • Make every color spell a real word. There is only one solution, and all four words fall into place at the same moment.
  • Striped tiles are already correct and never change. The four corners always start striped.
  • The theme above the grid is your clue.

Controls

  • Tap a tile to outline its pinwheel. The letters it controls are highlighted in the words under the grid. Tap it again to turn it.
  • Hint sets one pinwheel for you and stripes it.
  • Undo takes back your last turn. Reset puts the colors back where they started.
  • Par is the fewest turns that solve the puzzle. Your turns are counted under the clock. Undo does not take a turn off the count.

Tips

  • Start with the theme. Guess a word that fits it, then look for its letters in reading order.
  • Use the corners. The top-left letter starts the coral word and the bottom-right letter ends the green word. They never change.
  • Top rows are beginnings, bottom rows are endings. A letter in the top row is near the start of its word. One in the bottom row is near the end.

Why "Pinwheel"?

It's an actual secret code. Pinwheel comes from the Turning Grille, a cipher used to hide messages for hundreds of years. A card with holes was laid over a grid of letters. The holes showed part of the message, and each quarter turn of the card showed more.
